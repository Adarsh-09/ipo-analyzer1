# IPOVision — Sources, Provenance & Analytical Limitations

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target Market:** Indian Mainboard IPOs Only

---

## 1. Data Sources & Provenance

IPOVision utilizes public, non-authenticated, regulatory, and secondary market data sources.

| Data Category | Target Domain / Source | Provenance & Extraction Methodology | Verifiability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Listing Outcomes** | NSE India (`nseindia.com`) & BSE India (`bseindia.com`) | Official historical trading records and listing announcements | High (Authoritative Exchange Data) |
| **Issue Structure & Filings** | SEBI (`sebi.gov.in`) | Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) & Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) filings | High (Statutory Filings) |
| **Final Subscriptions** | NSE & BSE Bidding Data | Aggregated bidding logs across QIB, NII, and Retail individual categories | High (Exchange Published) |
| **Grey Market Premium (GMP)** | Public IPO tracking aggregators (e.g. Chittorgarh, InvestorGain) | Web-scraped historical sentiment logs with timestamp and source attribution | Medium (Informal OTC Market) |
| **Market Index Benchmark** | Nifty 50 / BSE Sensex | Historical index closing levels leading up to subscription cutoff | High (Public Market Benchmark) |

## 2. Key Limitations & Risk Factors

### 1. The Nature of Grey Market Premium (GMP)
- **Informal & Unregulated:** Grey Market Premium represents informal forward contracts traded in unorganized dealer networks prior to exchange listing.
- **Illiquidity & Bias:** GMP quotes in thin trading can be volatile, exaggerated, or manipulated by market participants to create artificial demand.
- **Explicit Principle in IPOVision:** GMP is treated as a noisy sentiment signal, **never** an exchange-guaranteed price or financial truth. Missing GMP values are explicitly flagged and must never be defaulted to zero.

### 2. Sample Size & Historical Depth
- Indian Mainboard IPO issuance is cyclical: bull markets see 60–90 listings annually, whereas bear markets may produce fewer than 20.
- Over a 5-to-7 year historical span, the total sample size comprises approximately 300 to 500 mainboard issues.
- **Methodological Safeguard:** Small sample sizes prohibit high-parameter deep learning or unconstrained architectures. We rely on regularized models (Ridge, Logistic Regression with L1/L2 penalties) and constrained tree ensembles (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting) with walk-forward testing.

### 3. Excluded Asset Classes (SME IPOs)
- **Scope Exclusion:** BSE SME and NSE Emerge IPOs are intentionally excluded from Version 1.0. SME issues operate under lower listing capital requirements, 100% retail underwriting guarantees, higher minimum lot sizes (often ₹1-1.5 Lakh per lot), and restricted secondary market liquidity, requiring a fundamentally different predictive framework.

### 4. Accounting Nuances & Restated Financials
- Offer documents present "Restated Financial Statements" under Ind AS, which may differ from statutory balance sheets published during subsequent annual reports.
- Issue timelines frequently feature stub periods (e.g., 6-month or 9-month audited figures). All revenue and profit metrics must be carefully documented to avoid false year-over-year distortion.
